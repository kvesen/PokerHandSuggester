Poker Hand Suggester — Your Pocket Texas Hold'em Strategy Partner

Stop guessing at the table. Poker Hand Suggester puts mathematically optimal Fold/Call/Raise decisions in your pocket, powered by real Monte Carlo equity simulation running entirely on your device.

HOW IT WORKS

Enter your hole cards and community cards manually, or scan them with your camera (coming soon — on-device ML). The app instantly runs thousands of Monte Carlo equity simulations to calculate your true winning probability against all opponent hand ranges.

Combined with pot odds calculation and expected value (EV) math, the decision engine tells you exactly what to do:
• FOLD — when continuing costs more than your equity justifies
• CALL — when pot odds favour continuing
• RAISE — when your equity supports building the pot

KEY FEATURES

Monte Carlo Equity Simulation — 10,000+ runout simulations give you statistically accurate equity percentages against the full range of opponent holdings.

Pot Odds Calculator — Instantly computes pot odds from the current pot size and bet facing you, so you never lose track of whether a call is mathematically correct.

EV Computation — Expected value calculation factors in your equity, pot size, and call amount to give a clear verdict on every decision.

Position-Aware Thresholds — 9-seat position model adjusts decision thresholds based on where you sit at the table (UTG, MP, CO, BTN, BB), reflecting real game theory.

Hand History — Review your past decisions with a full session log stored privately on your device.

Dark & Light Mode — Polished dark and light themes for comfortable use in any environment.

Privacy-First Design — All processing happens on your device. No accounts, no data collection, no ads. Your hand history never leaves your phone.

On-Device ML Card Scanning (Coming Soon) — TensorFlow Lite-powered camera scanner will recognise cards automatically — no manual entry needed.

WHO IS THIS FOR?

Whether you're a beginner learning pot odds for the first time or an experienced player who wants a second opinion in home games, Poker Hand Suggester gives you the mathematical edge — without requiring you to memorise equity charts or run mental arithmetic under pressure.
